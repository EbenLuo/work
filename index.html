A brief history of input

How we played.

Game history is often told as a story of better graphics. This site tells a different story: the biggest changes came from the ways we touched, moved, and connected to games.

Explore the eras

Not a march of better graphics.

From the arcade cabinet to the living room, from the analog stick to the touchscreen, each era changed more than the picture on screen. It changed who could play, where they played, and what a game felt like.
