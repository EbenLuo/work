Project Plan

This file is for planning only. It is not one of the four website pages.

Five Ideas

  1. Play/Past: how new ways of playing changed games
  2. The Chinese internet café era, 1998–2008
  3. Handhelds: from Game Boy to Switch
  4. The rise and fall of the platformer
  5. Graphics: from 2D pixels to real-time 3D

Who Each Idea Is For

Play/Past
For students who are interested in game design and want to understand how games became what they are today.
The Chinese internet café era
For people who remember that time, and for younger players who want to learn about it.
Handhelds
For handheld players and people who like portable devices.
The platformer
For players who want to understand how a genre changes over time.
Graphics
For people who are curious about game engines and visual technology.

Why This Idea

I know games well, and I want to show that game history is not only a story about better graphics. It is also a story about new ways to play.

My Choice

Subtitle: A brief history of input, from joysticks to touchscreens.

Audience: Students who are interested in game design, and players who want to understand how games became what they are today.

Four pages: Home / The Arcade Era / The 3D Revolution / The Online Era.

The page that has to be best: Home, with the Playback year explorer.

Smallest version worth showing: Four pages, twelve credited historical photographs, and one Playback year explorer. No server, no database.

Image plan: Historical photographs from museum and official sources, saved locally and credited below each image and in CREDITS.md.

Visual direction: A game history archive: dark background, warm white text, green and amber labels, historical photographs on paper-coloured plates, and original icons for the archive identity.
