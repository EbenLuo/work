# Play/Past: How We Played

A brief history of input, from joysticks to touchscreens.

## Project

A student project for Web Technologies at BiUH, 2026.

The site explores three moments in game history when new ways of playing changed the medium:

- the move from arcades to the living room
- the arrival of 3D cameras and analog sticks
- the rise of online play and touchscreens

- The Arcade Era
- The 3D Revolution
- The Online Era

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No server, no database.
