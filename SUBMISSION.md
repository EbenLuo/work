## Title

Play/Past: How We Played

## One sentence

A short, visual history of how new ways of playing — from joysticks to
touchscreens — changed video games.

https://ebenluo.github.io/work/

## Description

Play/Past is a four-page site about the moments when a new way of interacting
with a game changed the medium. It is not a complete timeline. It argues that
the important changes in game history were not only about better graphics, but
about the joystick, the analog stick, the network, and the touchscreen.

The site is divided into three eras. The Arcade Era follows games from
laboratory and public machines into the living room. The 3D Revolution looks at
how cameras and analog sticks made movement and looking part of the same
control problem. The Online Era follows the network and the touchscreen out of
the living room and into everyday life.

The home page contains **Playback**, a year explorer that turns the timeline
into something the visitor can use. Moving the slider selects the nearest
exhibit and updates the title, description, era link, and an accessible status
message. The interaction is plain JavaScript and runs entirely in the browser.

The design uses a dark archive palette, warm paper-coloured image plates, and
museum photographs. Historical images are downloaded locally and credited under
each image and in `CREDITS.md`. The site uses no frameworks, no build step, no
server-side code, and no database.
